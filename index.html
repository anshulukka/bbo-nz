Questions

Common questions from NZ advisers

Do you understand New Zealand lending requirements?

Yes. Our Auckland team works with NZ lenders and application requirements every day, and our processes are built around how New Zealand advisers operate. You stay responsible for your advice; we make sure the file is complete, accurate and ready for you.

Will my clients know someone else is helping?

Only if you want them to. Many advisers have us work behind the scenes; others introduce us as part of their team. It's your call.

How is my clients' information kept secure?

We work in the systems you already use, with controlled access and documented processes, and we treat personal information in line with the NZ Privacy Act 2020. Ask us for details on the call.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. You can start with a single application on a pay-per-application basis, then scale up to a dedicated team member if it suits you.

What are your support hours?

Our Auckland office is open Monday to Friday, 8:30 am to 5:00 pm NZ time, and the wider team keeps work moving outside those hours.

What does it cost?

It depends on the service and volume. Book a free call and we'll give you a clear, no-pressure quote.
