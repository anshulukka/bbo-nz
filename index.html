About Brokers' BackOffice

Australia's leading broker back-office service, now in New Zealand

Brokers' BackOffice was founded in Sydney to solve a simple problem: mortgage brokers spend too much time on paperwork and not enough time with clients. We built a team and a process that takes loan processing, admin and paraplanning off your plate — at a reasonable, per-file fee with no fixed costs and no long-term commitment.

We have been operating in Australia since 2015 and are trusted by 120+ broking businesses. In 2026 we opened our Auckland office to bring the same service to New Zealand mortgage advisers — built from the ground up for how NZ lending actually works, with resources trained on NZ lender portals, MyCRM, FileInvite and CCCFA documentation requirements.

Common questions

Questions from NZ mortgage advisers

Do you understand New Zealand lending and compliance requirements?

Yes. Our Auckland team and our NZ-trained processors work with NZ lender portals and application requirements every day. Our processes are built around CCCFA documentation obligations, the Financial Markets Conduct Act and how NZ advisers operate under their FAP licence. You remain responsible for your advice; we make sure the file is complete, accurate and lodgement-ready.

What is the difference between Pay Per File and a Dedicated Resource?

Pay Per File is flexible — you send us files as they come in and pay only for what we process. It suits advisers who want to trial the service or who have variable volume. A Dedicated Resource is a named team member who works alongside you on a set number of hours each week — they learn your lenders, your systems and your preferences and work as an extension of your team. We can help you decide which is right for your business on the first call.

Which platforms and CRMs does your team work in?

Our NZ team is trained on MyCRM (NZFSG), FileInvite, Salesforce and the major NZ lender portals including ASB, ANZ, BNZ, Westpac and Kiwibank. If you use a different platform, let us know on the call — we can usually accommodate it.

Will my clients know someone else is helping with their file?

Only if you want them to. Many advisers prefer us to work in the background; others introduce us as part of their support team. It is entirely your call.

How is my clients' information kept secure?

Brokers' BackOffice is ISO 27001 certified — we operate documented access controls and security processes. We work within the systems you already use and handle personal information in line with the NZ Privacy Act 2020. We are happy to walk you through our security arrangements on the call.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. You can start with a single file on a pay-per-file basis and scale from there. There is no lock-in period and no minimum commitment.

What does it cost?

Fees depend on the service and your volume. We do not publish a standard rate card because every business is different — book a free call and we will give you a clear, no-pressure quote.
